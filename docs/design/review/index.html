How the numbers are made. Quasi-static: a vertical ground reaction at the foot, standing on four legs (¼ weight each, held continuously → allowance 25 % of stall) and a trot peak on two legs (½ weight × 1.5 dynamic factor, swept over the step → allowance 60 % of stall). Hip torque = force × fore-aft foot offset; knee torque = force × horizontal knee-to-foot offset; abad torque = force × (abad link + 10 mm lateral shift). Speeds come from inverse kinematics along the gait cycle, compared with the servo firmware's 5.29 rad/s target-rate cap (Rhoban BAM identification). Mass is a component model: 12 × 55 g servos, measured electronics, and a parametric estimate of printed parts.

    
Servo geometry measured from the STS3215 case meshes in Open Duck Mini v2 (45.2 × 24.7 × 35.7 mm, shaft 9.6 mm from the end). Belt ratios use GT2 tooth counts; the pushrod ratio is an average — the real 4-bar varies about ±20 % over the knee range.
