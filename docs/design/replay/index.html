Data table for this run (every 0.5 s)

What was simulated. Each run stands for 1 s on the keyframe pose, then plays 6 s of gait targets from the design library's inverse kinematics. Feet are 10 mm spheres with friction 1.0 on a flat floor; a fall is a trunk below half the stance height or more than 46° of pitch or roll.

How to read it. Open-loop speed below the command is feet slipping and the trunk pitching under the rear legs' push — the front feet float most of the cycle (gait diagram). The leveled run shows how much one cheap correction recovers; the RL policy closes the rest. Torque saturating at the clamp during swing is the stiff firmware loop hitting its limit for a few samples, not a design margin problem — the standing knee torque of matches the 0.22 N·m quasi-static estimate.
